## Consent

By providing your mobile number through this website or when speaking with us, you agree that we may contact you by text message about your inquiry, your inspection and the work you have asked about.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase. You can ask us to call rather than text at any time.

## What We Send

Messages relating to your specific inquiry: confirming an appointment, letting you know somebody is on the way, following up after an inspection, or answering a question you have asked.

We do not send marketing campaigns, promotional blasts or newsletters by text.

## Frequency

Message frequency varies and depends entirely on the conversation you are having with us. In most cases it is a handful of messages around a single appointment.

## Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. We do not charge for messages.

## Opting Out

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving texts. You will get one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and nothing after that.

Opting out of texts does not remove you from anything else. We can still call you about your inquiry unless you ask us not to.

Reply **HELP** for assistance, or call **(256) 270-0146**.

## Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy

Your mobile number is handled as described in our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/). It is not sold or passed to marketing lists.
