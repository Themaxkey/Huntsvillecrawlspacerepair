## What This Policy Covers

This policy explains what information Huntsville Crawl Space Repair collects through huntsvillecrawlspacerepair.com, why we collect it, and who it is shared with.

## Information We Collect

When you submit the contact form we collect the name, phone number, email address and message you provide. We do not require any information beyond what is needed to respond to your inquiry.

When you call the number on this site, your phone number is recorded as part of normal call handling, and calls may be recorded or transcribed so that we do not lose the details of a request.

Our hosting provider records standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. This is used to keep the site working and secure.

## How Your Information Is Used

To respond to your inquiry, arrange an inspection, and follow up about the work you asked about. That is the whole purpose.

## Who It Is Shared With

Requests submitted through this website are shared with local, licensed and insured crawl space and foundation contractors serving your area, who contact you directly using the details you provide.

We do not sell your information, and we do not pass it to marketing lists or to multiple competing companies.

We use third-party services to run the site and deliver messages, including our website host and our email delivery provider. These handle your data only in order to provide those services.

## How Long We Keep It

Inquiry details are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you get back in touch. Ask us to delete your details and we will.

## Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our host records basic aggregate traffic information for security and performance.

## Your Choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Contact us using the details on the contact page.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
