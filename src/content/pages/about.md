## How This Site Works

Huntsville Crawl Space Repair connects homeowners across Huntsville, Madison County and the wider Tennessee Valley with local, licensed and insured crawl space and foundation contractors. When you call the number on this site or send a request through the form, your details go to a local contractor who contacts you directly to arrange an inspection.

We would rather say that plainly here than bury it in small print. You are not being routed through a call center, and your details are not sold on to a list of competing companies who all then ring you at dinner.

## What We Care About Getting Right

**Finding the water before selling the cure.** Nearly every crawl space problem in this region traces back to moisture, and moisture has four possible sources — surface runoff, groundwater, plumbing and condensation. They need four different fixes. Encapsulating a crawl space that floods, or draining one whose problem is humid summer air, is money spent on the wrong thing.

**Measuring rather than guessing.** Wood decay begins above roughly twenty percent moisture content. That is a number, it takes seconds to read, and it turns an argument about whether there is a problem into a fact. Any assessment that does not include it is an opinion.

**Telling people when the answer is cheap.** A meaningful share of the crawl spaces we look at need downspout extensions and a liner, not a five-figure job. Saying so costs us work and it is the reason anyone should trust the times we say otherwise.

## What We Will Not Do

We will not quote structural work over the phone as though it were a fixed product.

We will not tell you a crawl space can be made permanently free of mold. Spores exist in all air; what can be controlled is moisture, and controlling it is what actually stops growth.

We will not sell piers for a floor that is bouncing because its interior supports have settled. Those are different problems with very different prices, and confusing them is the most expensive mistake in this trade.

And we will not lead with a frightening lab report before anyone has established where the water is coming from.

## Why Huntsville

Two reasons this work is more common here than most places.

The climate. Summer dew points in the low seventies for months, meeting framing that sits nearer sixty-five degrees. Vented crawl spaces were required by code for decades and in this climate they function as a moisture delivery system rather than a cure.

The geology. Limestone under a clay layer, dissolved through with joints and channels, which means groundwater does not always behave the way it would further south, and a crawl space can be dry one week and wet the next with no rain in between.

Neither is bad luck. Both are predictable, which is also why they are worth understanding rather than panicking about.
