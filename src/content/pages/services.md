Ten services, and they belong in an order. Most crawl space problems in this region are a chain — water gets in, humidity stays high, wood decays, supports fail, floors move — and fixing a link out of sequence means paying twice.

## Start Here: Stop the Water

**[Crawl Space Waterproofing](/crawl-space-waterproofing/)** deals with liquid water. Interior perimeter drainage, sump systems, and the exterior corrections that solve a surprising share of cases for a few hundred dollars.

**[Sump Pump Installation](/sump-pump-installation/)** is the last stage of that drainage: collecting water at a low point and lifting it out of the house properly.

## Then: Control the Air

**[Vapor Barrier Installation](/vapor-barrier-installation/)** covers the ground, which in an unlined crawl space is the single largest moisture source — bare soil can release several gallons a day.

**[Crawl Space Encapsulation](/crawl-space-encapsulation/)** goes further: sealed vents, liner up the walls, insulation moved to the perimeter, and a dehumidifier. In the Tennessee Valley this is the change that fixes damp, smell and cold floors together.

**[Crawl Space Moisture Control](/crawl-space-moisture-control/)** is the ongoing part — keeping wood below the twenty percent moisture content where decay becomes active, and being able to see that it is working.

## Then: Repair the Damage

**[Crawl Space Repair](/crawl-space-repair/)** covers decayed joists and girders, failed supports, and the sagging floors they produce.

**[Crawl Space Mold Removal](/crawl-space-mold-removal/)** removes what has grown and treats what remains — worth nothing at all unless the moisture work happens too.

**[Crawl Space Insulation](/crawl-space-insulation/)** puts insulation where it survives in this climate, which is the perimeter walls rather than between the floor joists.

## Separately: Structure and Basements

**[Foundation Repair](/foundation-repair/)** addresses movement in what the house sits on — settlement on fill, seasonal clay movement, and the localized settlement that limestone country occasionally produces.

**[Basement Waterproofing](/basement-waterproofing/)** for the minority of Huntsville houses with a basement, usually daylight or walkout basements cut into a slope.
